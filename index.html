DMV weather is hard on pianos.

Humid summers swell a piano's wooden soundboard; dry, heated winters shrink it. Those seasonal swings pull the strings off pitch — even if nobody is playing.

Most pianos in the region stay sounding their best with a tuning once or twice a year, ideally a few weeks after the heat or air conditioning settles in for the season.

About

A small, independent piano service based in Fairfax County.

We tune and care for pianos across Washington DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia — one home, school or church at a time.

Why “Smyrna”?

Smyrna is the ancient name of İzmir, a port city on Turkey's Aegean coast with a long love of music. We borrowed it as a reminder of where good sound starts: patience, care and a well-tuned instrument.

How we work

A piano is often the most valuable — and most personal — thing in a room. We treat it that way, and we treat your home the same: shoe covers on, a drop cloth down, and everything put back as we found it.

We tune by ear, checked with an electronic tuning device, and take the time to set each string so the tuning holds through the season instead of drifting in a few weeks.

If a piano isn't worth the cost of a repair, we'll say so. Honest advice is part of the service.
